Frequently Asked Questions

Below are answers to some of our most commonly asked questions.

Who can enrol?

Our mock examinations are designed primarily for Year 12 students preparing for the NSW HSC. High-achieving Year 11 students are also welcome to enrol.

What subjects are offered?

We currently offer:

• Mathematics Advanced
• Mathematics Standard
• English Advanced
• English Standard
• Biology
• Chemistry
• Physics
• Mathematics Extension 1

How much does each package cost?

Each subject package costs $299.95 and includes:

✔ Two full HSC-style examinations
✔ Tutor marking and written feedback
✔ Online tutor consultation
✔ Grade report
✔ Topic-by-topic analysis
✔ Ranking amongst students
✔ Time management analysis
✔ Personalised revision plan

When do registrations open and close?

Registrations open on 3 August and close on 30 August.

When are the examinations held?

The Term 1 mock examination period runs from 5th September until 13 September. Exams are conducted on Saturdays and Sundays.

Will I receive an exam timetable?

Yes. Once you have registered, you'll receive a personalised examination timetable based on the subjects you've enrolled in.

How are the exams marked?

Every examination is professionally marked by experienced tutors using HSC-style marking criteria. Detailed written feedback is provided for every paper.

What is included in the Grade Report?

Your personalised report includes:

• Overall mark
• Topic-by-topic strengths
• Areas needing improvement
• Ranking among students
• Time management analysis
• Personalised revision plan

Who is the lead tutor?

TopMark Academy is led by Samaah Singh, who achieved:

• 98.95 ATAR
• Band 6 in Mathematics Advanced
• Band 6 in English Advanced
• Band 6 in Biology

How do I contact TopMark Academy?

Simply visit our Contact page and send us an enquiry. We'll respond as soon as possible.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is happy to answer any questions you may have before registering.
