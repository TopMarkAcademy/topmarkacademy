What's Included

1. Two HSC-Style Examination Sessions

Build confidence under real exam conditions with two full-length HSC-style mock examinations, scheduled one week apart. Between each exam, you'll receive detailed feedback and actionable advice, allowing you to improve before sitting your second paper and tracking your progress.

2. Detailed Marking

Every response is marked against an HSC-standard marking rubric, with insights informed by current HSC marking practices. Receive comprehensive annotations, clear explanations for lost marks, and practical strategies to maximise your performance.

3. Comprehensive Performance Report

  • Strengths and areas for improvement
  • Topic-by-topic analysis of your understanding and progress
  • Performance compared with other students in the cohort
  • Personalised recommendations for future revision

4. One-on-One Tutor Consultation

Meet individually with your tutor to review your results in detail. Together you'll identify strengths, address areas for improvement and develop a personalised study plan with clear, achievable goals leading into your HSC examinations.
